## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 01. [E-Commerce Customer Churn Prediction](https://bnyuu.github.io)
> **Machine Learning • Explainable AI (XAI)**
- Evaluated models across **50,000 customer profiles** with training-fold SMOTE class resampling.
- **Performance**: Testing Accuracy **90.34%** | Macro F1 **0.828** | ROC-AUC **0.923**.
- Extracted key churn drivers using SHAP feature importance (*Customer Service Calls, LTV, Cart Abandonment*).
- **Stack**: `Python` • `Scikit-learn` • `Random Forest` • `SMOTE` • `SHAP` • `Pandas`

---

### 02. [SmartSawit: IoT & AI-Powered Smart Agriculture](https://bnyuu.github.io)
> **IoT Embedded Systems • Telemetry Dashboard**
- Real-time soil moisture and climate telemetry monitoring using **ESP32**, **DHT11**, and **Soil Hygrometer**.
- Automated 5V pump control targeting **5–10% reduction in water & fertilizer costs**.
- Real hardware prototype with 6x oil palm saplings and precision drip tubing.
- **Stack**: `ESP32` • `C++ / Arduino` • `FastAPI` • `React` • `Relay 5V` • `Sensors`

---

### 03. [AI Literacy, Academic Integrity & Digital Trust](https://bnyuu.github.io)
> **Academic Quantitative Research • Empirical Analytics**
- Surveyed **$N = 213$ undergraduates** in Bekasi City to measure generative AI adoption.
- Statistical regression proving AI literacy strongly predicts Academic Integrity ($R^2 = 0.842$) and Digital Trust ($R^2 = 0.843$).
- **Stack**: `IBM SPSS` • `Structural Equation Modeling (SEM)` • `Regression Analysis` • `Python`
